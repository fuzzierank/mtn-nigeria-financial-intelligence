# MTN Nigeria: 2023–2024 Financial Stress Analysis

## Core finding

MTN's 2024 revenue increased **36.1%**, but operating expenses increased **76.6%**. EBITDA still grew **9.2%** to ₦1.313tn.

The major below-EBITDA pressures were:
- 2024 net finance costs: **₦403.2bn**
- 2024 net foreign exchange loss: **₦925.4bn**
- 2024 PAT: **-₦400.4bn**

The 2024 annual report attributes the FX loss to the revaluation of foreign-currency obligations after the naira moved from about ₦907/$ at end-2023 to ₦1,535/$ at end-2024.

## H1 versus H2

H1 2024:
- PAT: **-₦519.1bn**
- Net FX loss: **₦887.7bn**
- EBITDA margin: **35.6%**

H2 2024:
- PAT: **₦118.6bn**
- Net FX loss: **₦37.7bn**
- EBITDA margin: **42.1%**

This shows a sharp second-half recovery. MTN also reported tower-contract renegotiation savings of approximately ₦113.8bn and broader expense-efficiency savings of about ₦42bn.

## 2025 recovery

In 2025, MTN reported a **₦90.3bn FX gain** versus a **₦925.4bn FX loss** in 2024, while PAT recovered to **₦1.113tn**.

## Analytical interpretation

The evidence supports a distinction between:
1. **Underlying commercial/operating momentum**, which remained positive;
2. **Operating cost pressure**, which weakened margins;
3. **FX and finance effects**, which had an outsized impact on reported profit.
